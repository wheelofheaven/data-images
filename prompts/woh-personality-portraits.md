# Wheel of Heaven Personality Portraits

Purpose: project-bound wiki and library metadata portraits for figures repeatedly referenced across `www.wheelofheaven.world`.
Generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-06-21 and persisted from the Codex session log into `data-images/raw/portraits/`.
Use these prompts to regenerate or revise the files if the visual direction changes.

Raw destination: `data-images/raw/portraits/`
Manifest category: `wiki`
Output size: square, 1254 x 1254 PNG source files.

## Shared Style

- Editorial painted portrait, semi-realistic, scholarly wiki tone.
- Square 1:1 crop, readable as a small metadata thumbnail.
- Restrained lighting, natural anatomy, subtle fine grain.
- No text, logo, watermark, devotional halo, or explicit sacred symbol.
- These are interpretive concept portraits, not documentary likenesses.

## Revision Notes: 2026-06-23 v2

Generated v2 variants for Rael and the Elohim cast after review against local source material:

- `data-content/wiki/rael.md` identifies Rael as Claude Vorilhon, a 27-year-old French motor-sports journalist at the 1973 Puy-de-Lassolas contact.
- `data-content/wiki/rael.md` summarizes the contact figure as approximately 1.20 meters tall, with long dark hair, a beard, pale slightly olive-tinted skin, large almond-shaped eyes, and a white single-piece suit.
- `data-content/wiki/yahweh.md`, `data-content/wiki/lucifer.md`, and `data-content/wiki/satan.md` distinguish Yahweh, Lucifer, and Satan as separate Eloha figures rather than supernatural/demonic types.
- `data-library/lets-welcome-the-extraterrestrials/tts/chapter-2.en.json` frames Satan as an Eloha political opposition leader and Lucifer as a light-bearing Eloha scientist who loved and educated the first humans.

The v2 prompts therefore make Rael closer to public 1970s Claude Vorilhon imagery without copying a specific photograph, and align the Elohim cast around a shared visual language: compact stature implied by environment, pale olive skin, large almond-shaped eyes, long dark hair for male figures, and seamless white advanced garments with restrained role accents.

## Revision Notes: 2026-06-24 v3

Generated a narrow v3 pass for two assets only:
